JoltDrum · Privacy policy

Privacy

Effective November 11, 2026Spark & Grow Music LLC

JoltDrum does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only connection it makes is to Apple's App Store, to show kit pack prices, make a purchase or restore purchases.

JoltDrum is an app for iPhone made by Spark & Grow Music LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

What stays on your device

The app keeps your settings, such as the kit, colours, tempo and mixer levels, in its own storage on your device so they are there next time you open it. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

The app may also keep performance and crash reports provided by Apple's operating system (MetricKit) in its own storage on your device. The app does not send these to us or to anyone else.

Crash reports through Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

MIDI

If you connect a MIDI device, JoltDrum sends and receives clock and start/stop messages with it. This stays between your device and the equipment you connect.

Microphone, camera and location

JoltDrum does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Purchases

Any purchase of JoltDrum or within it is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

JoltDrum does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
