Quick answers

I can't hear anything.

Turn the volume up, check whether the sound is going to the speaker, headphones or a Bluetooth device, then press Play.

Do I need MIDI gear?

No, MIDI clock is optional and JoltDrum keeps its own time by default.

Do I need an account?

No, there is no sign-in, JoltDrum works offline and collects no data (see the privacy policy).

Where are the tutorials?

A walkthrough plays on first launch, and you can replay the tutorials any time from OPTIONS.
